What I take on

Three kinds of engagement. All of them end with a document your team keeps.

Automation architecture reviews

A read of your n8n instance or whatever is running in its place: workflow structure, error handling, credential hygiene, queue mode, what happens when a node fails at 2am. You get findings ordered by what will cost you first.

AI voice-agent readiness

Before you buy or build: which calls are actually automatable, what your telephony and data setup will support, where the latency budget goes, and what a realistic first deployment looks like. Vendor-neutral.

Workshops

Half-day and full-day sessions for engineering and ops teams on n8n, agent design, and evaluating AI vendors. Built around your workflows, not slides.
