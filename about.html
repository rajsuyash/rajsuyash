03

My role in an engagement

Understand the process

I work with the person responsible for the workflow to establish the inputs, decisions, systems and exceptions. A useful scope says what the system is allowed to do and what stays with the team.

Design and implement

The work can include workflow design, integration code, agent instructions and evaluation cases. Your proposal identifies the deliverables and any other people or providers involved rather than leaving responsibility implied.

Make the handoff usable

Agree who owns the accounts, documentation, monitoring and recovery. Ongoing operation needs an explicit scope. Your team should be able to locate a failed run and understand the next step.

I offer remote consulting and implementation for teams in India, the US and Europe from my base in Paris. We agree time-zone overlap, access requirements and the delivery scope before starting. I do not claim a local office in each market.

How can I evaluate your work before a call?

Start with the linked engineering guides and project accounts. They show the implementation topics I work on and the reasoning behind them. For a project decision, agree a relevant demonstration and acceptance criteria rather than assuming a published case predicts your result.
