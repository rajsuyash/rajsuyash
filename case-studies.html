Turn a relevant example into a project brief

Define the work

Write down the trigger, required input, permitted actions and target system. Identify who owns the workflow and which decisions must stay with a person.

Agree the evidence

For a proposed outcome, agree the baseline, measurement window and definition of success before launch. Include failures, escalations and operating cost in the assessment.
